Democracy 2.0 / Coolbrador

The kennel decides. LabradorSim rehearses it.

Coolbrador is a kennel with democracy: Labradors propose, poll, and steer what the site becomes (features, moderation norms, experiments) while AI helps evolve the product from what wins. LabradorSim is the civic lab next door: stress-test scenarios before they hit the live kennel.
