Community rules

These rules apply to posts, drawings, profiles, polls and messages, including posts shared with Chipper.

  • No threats, targeted harassment, hateful attacks or encouragement of violence.
  • No child sexual exploitation or abuse material, grooming, or sexual content involving children. Never upload or forward suspected abuse material to make a report.
  • No intimate images shared without consent, doxxing, stalking, or sharing someone else's private information without permission.
  • No scams, impersonation intended to deceive, malware or spam.
  • Share only content you have the right to publish. Respect copyright and other people's privacy.
  • Do not post illegal content. A content warning does not make prohibited content acceptable.

Content that violates these rules may be removed. A moderator reviews reports; a report alone does not prove a violation. Automated media checks may assist with uploads, but can make mistakes and do not guarantee that content is safe.

Control what you see

Use a post's options menu to report it or block its author. Blocking hides their posts in feeds and prevents messages and friend requests between your accounts. Public posts can still be viewed while signed out. Manage blocks in Friends.

In Settings, mute words and phrases on this device. Sensitive posts start collapsed unless you choose to show them. You can reveal individual posts without changing your preference.

Report suspected illegal content

You do not need an account. Prepare a notice below, then send it using your email app to support@coolbrador.com. Preparing a draft does not send or submit a report. If your email app does not open, copy the draft and email it yourself.

For an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact local emergency services. Do not attach illegal material; provide the exact link and a description.

Your contact details belong in this private notice, not in a public post.

Ask for a review

If you disagree with a decision, email support@coolbrador.com with its reference, the content link, and why you think it was mistaken. Do not send your password or re-upload removed illegal material. You can also contact the relevant Digital Services Coordinator or seek judicial redress; applicable out-of-court options depend on the service and decision.

How your feed works

The Latest feed shows public posts and reposts ordered by posting or reposting time, newest first. Friends limits that feed to your accepted friends and you. A community page limits it to that community. Blocks hide authors from these feeds. Search in the feed filters the posts already loaded; Load more fetches older posts.

The public search page matches complete words from profiles and posts. Poll charts show recorded vote shares over time. They are not betting odds and you cannot wager money here.

BeeSid posts can appear in Chipper alongside its preserved game archive. Posts, drawings, replies, profiles, reactions and poll votes are public. Messages are visible to conversation participants; they are not end-to-end encrypted.
