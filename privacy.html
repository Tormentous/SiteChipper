What is public?

Your display name, avatar, biography, posts, drawings, replies, reposts, reactions, communities and poll votes are public. BeeSid posts may also appear in Chipper. Avoid publishing addresses, phone numbers or other sensitive personal information. Public content may be copied by other people.

What is private?

Your sign-in email and account credentials are used for account access. Conversations are available to their participants. Your block list and notification read state are private account data. Reports are available to moderators; report outcomes are shared with the relevant reporter and author without exposing the reporter's identity to the author.

Storage on this device

Sign-in uses browser storage to keep you logged in. Appearance, wallpaper, content preferences, muted words, chosen feed interests, recent seen-post IDs and drafts are stored on this device. Drafts are not published until you submit them. Clearing browser storage removes local preferences and unpublished drafts. Sign out when you finish on a shared device.

Services involved

Firebase provides account authentication, database, file storage and hosting. Pages load scripts or fonts from Google and Cloudflare, and opening linked external services contacts those services. Uploaded media may be sent to the configured safety scanning provider for review. The account download does not include provider service logs or backups.

Your choices and requests

Edit your public profile on your profile page. Change your email or password, download your account content, or request account deletion in Settings. Deletion locks the account while its data is removed. Other people's messages and communities remain. Copies held outside Coolbrador are not removed by this process.

For access beyond the downloadable copy, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, or questions about processing, contact support@coolbrador.com. Include enough information to locate your account, but never your password. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

Privacy notice status
