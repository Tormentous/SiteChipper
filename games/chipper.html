PLAY as a Labrador Retriever!

ONE MAGIC BUTTERFLY. ONE TV-AD DOG TOY. DESTINY.

You PLAY as an AWESOME Labrador Retriever from the island town of Labradoria who notices an ad on TV about a SUPER COOL dog toy, where the price is just ONE of those MAGIC butterflies!

USE SUPER COOL tools that can DIG up shovels, EAT furniture, CAPTURE MAGIC butterflies and who knows what else!

EVERY MISSION IS UNIQUE

You will be traveling and even through different times encountering all sorts of enemies, from a metallic Labrador who is completely overpowered, bought by a Utah man controlling the bidding market, to a self-proclaimed superintellegent middleground pig who... well, I can't tell you what he does yet but BEWARE OF HIM!!
